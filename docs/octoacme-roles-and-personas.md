# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape the end-user experience and ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned to user needs. They translate customer pain points and product goals into clear interaction patterns, visual design, and usability feedback.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or contribute to user research and usability validation
- Define user flows, wireframes, and design patterns
- Partner with Product Managers on prioritization and customer value
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to validate feasibility and acceptance criteria
- Ensure design consistency across features and touchpoints

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework by validating design decisions early
- Create experiences that are accessible, clear, and testable

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and roadmap discussions
- Design reviews with Developers and PMs
- Feedback loops with QA/Testing and stakeholders during validation

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Product Managers on problem framing and user outcomes
- Supports Developers by clarifying requirements and design intent
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on whether workflows and edge cases are testable
- Reports user insights and design risks to the Project Manager when they affect milestones or scope

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts connect stakeholder needs, process requirements, and delivery execution. They help teams turn ambiguous needs into clear requirements, workflows, and measurable business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements and process flows
- Translate stakeholder goals into backlog-ready user stories and acceptance criteria
- Identify dependencies, process gaps, and operational constraints
- Support planning and prioritization discussions with Product and Project Managers
- Help validate that delivered work meets business intent

### Goals
- Improve requirement clarity and reduce ambiguity
- Align delivery work to stakeholder outcomes and business processes
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood requirements

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops
- Backlog refinement with Product Managers and Developers
- Status updates with Project Managers on scope and dependencies

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to frame the problem and success metrics
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify requirements and expected behaviors
- Keeps Project Managers informed of business dependencies, change requests, and approval needs
- Helps coordinate between stakeholders and delivery teams during planning and rollout

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide system design, technical standards, and implementation quality. They balance delivery speed with long-term maintainability, integration health, and technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, integration patterns, and design guardrails
- Review technical trade-offs and resolve design ambiguities
- Guide implementation decisions and code quality standards
- Identify cross-team dependencies, scalability concerns, and technical risks
- Support release planning and deployment readiness for complex changes

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid brittle integrations
- Support predictable implementation across the team

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency planning with Project Managers and Product Leads
- Working sessions with Developers during implementation and troubleshooting

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to align technical feasibility with business goals
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery risks, sequencing concerns, and dependency impacts
- Provides technical direction to Developers without removing ownership from the implementation team
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on critical workflows, risk areas, and production-readiness checks

---

## DevOps / Release Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers enable reliable delivery, automation, and operational readiness. They help teams move from code to production with a lower risk of deployment failure and faster recovery from issues.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, environments, and automation for builds and deployments
- Support release planning, rollback plans, and production readiness checks
- Monitor health signals such as deployments, logs, and infrastructure state
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing on environment configuration and smoke tests
- Help document operational runbooks and incident response steps when relevant

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and time-to-release
- Improve service reliability and recovery speed
- Create repeatable, observable delivery workflows

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness reviews
- CI/CD and environment status reports
- Coordination with on-call, support, and project leadership during high-risk releases

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate environment readiness and deployment workflows
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on staging validation and release smoke tests
- Supports Project Managers by tracking release timing, rollback readiness, and operational dependencies
- Provides Product Managers and stakeholders with release status and risk information when changes affect customer-facing delivery

---

## Customer Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring real-world usage signals back into the project lifecycle. They help validate customer impact, capture recurring issues, and ensure team decisions consider operational realities.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer issues, incident patterns, and feedback
- Help define support readiness and communication needs for releases
- Clarify operational constraints and service expectations
- Work with Product and Project Managers on post-release follow-up
- Identify opportunities to improve reliability, process, and customer experience

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and issue resolution speed
- Reduce operational surprises during go-live and ongoing support
- Ensure project decisions reflect real support and service experience

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness and post-release reviews
- Incident and support escalation updates
- Customer feedback summaries and operational trend reports

### Interaction with existing roles
- Feeds customer pain points and support trends to Product Managers and Project Managers
- Helps Developers and QA/Testing understand edge cases and real-world usage scenarios
- Supports stakeholder communication by surfacing operational impact and readiness signals
- Contributes to retrospectives and improvement planning after releases or incidents

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams can assign multiple responsibilities to a single person in smaller organizations, while larger teams should clearly separate ownership for speed, clarity, and accountability.
